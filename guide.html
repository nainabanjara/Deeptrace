GETTING STARTED

How to use DeepTrace

A simple guide for anyone who wants to check something suspicious.

01

Check a link

Open Link scanner, paste the website address and press Check this link. DeepTrace looks for unusual address patterns and attempts a basic website response check.

Example: example.com
02

Check a message

Open Message checker, paste the suspicious text and select Analyse message. The tool looks for common pressure tactics, requests for secret codes and unexpected payment language.

Tip: Remove your personal information first.
03

Investigate a domain

Open Domain investigator and enter a domain name. DeepTrace attempts to find its internet address and checks a few basic patterns.

Example: yourbank.com
04

Understand the risk score

Low: Few warning signs found. Medium: Some warning signs found. High: Several warning signs found. The score is a guide, not a guarantee.

A low score never means 100% safe.
05

Open and save reports

Every check is saved in the local database. Visit Investigation reports or Scan history to open, search, download or delete a report.

Reports can be downloaded as text files.
06

Use DeepTrace safely

Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs or CVVs. Do not paste private conversations. Confirm suspicious claims through official websites or phone numbers.

When in doubt, stop and verify independently.
07

Check a photo

Open Photo checker, upload a JPG, PNG or WEBP image, and review its dimensions, metadata, SHA-256 fingerprint and basic JPEG compression clues.

Important: these clues cannot prove a photo is fake or AI-generated.
Important: DeepTrace is an educational project that uses simple checks. It is not connected to police, banks or a live global scam database. It cannot guarantee that a link, domain or message is safe.
